01 / আমাদের দল

More than a band.
A feeling of home.

There’s something about the sound of a dhak. It brings back the excitement of Puja, familiar faces, and the joy of celebrating together.

That’s the feeling we bring to every performance. Holud Panjabi blends the traditional beat of the dhak with other instruments, keeping Bengali celebration at the heart of our music.

Now in our seventh year, we’re ready to make more memories. Especially on Dashami, when every beat carries a little extra emotion.
